# Wind Vane

## Notes
1. Fasten the face, lid, and enclosure parts with waterproof adhesive.
2. Secure AS5600 sensor with IC side facing shaft.
3. Cut 4mm rod to length.
4. Glue included magnet with AS5600 to bottom of 4mm rod.
5. Use M4 nuts, washsers, and bolt to move CoM of wind vane to the mounting hole.
6. Use M6 washer to act as spacer between inner and outer rotor parts.

Components
- (4) M4x8 bolts - for securing AS5600 to enclosure
- (4) M3x8 bolts - for bottom lid
- 4mm stainless steel rod cut to length - for axle of wind vane
- M4x(8mm-16mm) bolt - for balancing wind vane, may need extra nuts or washers (I used M4x12 bolt with (2) M4 nuts).
- (5) M4x5x5 heated, threaded inserts - 4 for AS5600 mounting and 1 for the balancing bolt of wind vane
- (4) M3x4x4.2 heated, threaded inserts - for the bottom cover of the wind vane
- (2) 8x4x3mm ball bearings - one in each divot of the inner rotor part.
- Support rod assembly (see support rod notes)
